Big Five Personality Inventory

Where do you fall on the five big traits of personality?

Psychologists describe personality with five broad traits, each made up of narrower facets. Rate 30 statements about yourself to see where you land on all five, what’s happening in the facets underneath, and what research says about your particular combination of traits.

  • Openness
  • Conscientiousness
  • Extraversion
  • Agreeableness
  • Neuroticism

Before you start

  • Describe yourself as you generally are now, not as you wish to be or as you are on an unusual day.
  • There are no right or wrong answers, and no trait is better than another. Every trait has advantages in some situations and costs in others.
  • Go with your first reaction. It takes about five minutes.

Your answers stay in this browser tab. Nothing is sent or stored anywhere unless you download or print your results. This is a tool for learning about personality psychology and reflecting on yourself, not a clinical or diagnostic assessment.
